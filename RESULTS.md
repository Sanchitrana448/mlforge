## Reading these numbers honestly

- The recommended threshold flags 524 of the 750 test customers. At a 10:1 cost ratio the cheapest policy is to contact most of the base. That is what the assumptions imply, not necessarily what a business would accept.
- The cost model assumes every retention offer works. Real offers save only some customers, which pushes the best threshold up.
- The data is synthetic with a known generating process, so absolute scores say little about production data.
